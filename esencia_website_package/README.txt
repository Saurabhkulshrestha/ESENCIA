Important before production launch
1. Final product photography in high-resolution web/marketplace sizes.
2. Final INCI lists and exact formula documentation for each SKU.
3. Final claims and substantiation; validate all percentages, efficacy and testing language.
4. SKU/UPC/GTIN, price, dimensions, net weight, country of origin, manufacturer/responsible party.
5. Final directions, warnings, storage, PAO/expiration, contact information and other label requirements.
6. Brand/legal pages: privacy, terms, returns/refunds, accessibility, cookie/analytics consent and contact details.
7. Ecommerce: cart, checkout, payments, tax, shipping, order confirmation and customer support.
8. Amazon: browse node/category, item type, search terms, variation strategy, compliance fields, Brand Registry and A+ eligibility.
9. SEO: canonical URLs, structured data, sitemap, robots, metadata, social sharing images, analytics and Search Console.
10. Reviews/testimonials and any expert or certification claims must be authentic and substantiated.

Deployment
